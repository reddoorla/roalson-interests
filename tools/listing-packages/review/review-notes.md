# Listing package review: Roalson Interests, 22 packages

Prepared September 29, 2026. These are the questions that came up while resetting the 22 packages in the new template. Figures in quotes are exactly as printed.

## Needs a decision before these go out

- **IH 10 at Menger Springs.** Size, price and zoning all differ from the website. The package says "10.2 acres" (3.8 + 6.4) and the website says "10.24 acres". The package prices Tract 1 at "$24.00 per square foot; $3,972,672" and Tract 2 at "$16.00 per square foot; $4,460,541", while the website says "Contact Broker". The package zoning is "City of Boerne, Texas" and the website's is "Kendall County".
- **The package price differs from the website on three listings.**
  - 101 W. Commerce St.: the package says "Contact Broker" and the website shows "$2,950,000" ($375.17/SF).
  - Cascade Caverns and Old San Antonio Road: the package says "$ 3,928,711.00; $18.00 per square foot" and the website says "Contact Broker".
  - 13810 Lookout Road, Suite B: the package says "$13.00/SF/YR NNN ($3.10/SF/YR Est)", and the website says "Contact Broker" and doesn't mark the space For Lease.
- **IH 10 at Highway 46.** The website lists this site **For Lease**. The package never says sale or lease, but it addresses "prospective buyers", so the new cover reads "For Sale". Which is right? The website's two highlights ("Site has excellent frontage and visibility.", "At entrance to Regent Park, a new 900 lot master planned residential development") also appear nowhere in the package.
- **IH 10 at Scenic Loop.** The package offers only "Lot 3: 1.369 Acre", but the website shows "2.09 acres". That is Lot 3 plus Lot 1 (0.72 ac), and the site plan now marks Lot 1 "SOLD SCOOTERS COFFEE". The website appears to predate that sale.
- **Menger Springs Commercial Development Sites.** The package says the property is "comprised of 6 lots", but Exhibit A shows 7 lots totalling 11.71 ac plus a 1.65-acre internal street. The "$2,618,827.20 or $4.50 per SF" price is exactly 13.36 ac × $4.50, so it charges for the street (about $323,433). Every lot on Exhibit A is also marked "B-1", while the package and the website say "C-1".
- **U.S. 69 (Hwy 77) and General Cavazos Blvd., Kingsville.** The package and the website say "zoned AG Agricultural". The included June 2017 survey says "ENTIRE TRACT IS ZONED 'C2'", and the November 2017 site plan says "EXISTING ZONING: AG, PROPOSED ZONING: C2". Please confirm the current zoning with the City.
- **Flood lines that don't match FEMA's current map.**
  - IH-35 at Wonderworld Drive: the package says "some floodplain along the frontage at IH-35", but about **45%** of the site is in the 100-year flood area.
  - 5001 Walzem Road has no flood line, yet about 5% of the lot is in the regulatory floodway, about 7% is in the 100-year area and about 55% is in the 500-year area.
  - 25331 IH 10 West has no flood line, yet about 40% of the lot is in the 100-year area.
- **5001 Walzem Road: lease terms.** The lease term reads "RENEWED: through Sept. 30, 2027", which leaves about a year, but the comments still say "Long term national credit tenant". The "7.0%" cap rate doesn't match the numbers shown: $109,800 ÷ $1,550,000 = 7.08%, and the rent has "annual increases", so the current figure may differ again.
- **25331 IH 10 West: availability.** The package offers office space from "+/- 975 sf to +/- 10,000 sf", and the website says "Up to 16,700 SF" / "Office Bldg: 975 - 10,000 SF". The package's own Office Building Detail marks Suites 105, 201 and 215 "Leased" and shows only 7,574 sf available (4,620 + 975 + 1,979).
- **IH-10 at FM 725, Seguin.** Comment 2 discloses environmental clean-up "estimating the clean-up to be $15,000 to $25,000". The website leaves this out and instead says "Seguin is a very business-friendly community.", which isn't in the package. Is the clean-up still outstanding, and which comments are current?
- **Contact details.**
  - 101 W. Commerce St. names "Matt Howard or Bart Wilson" but prints "eldon@roalson.com" and mhoward@roalson.com.
  - Loop 1604 at Dove Canyon names "Bart Wilson or Matt Howard" but prints bwilson@roalson.com and "eldon@roalson.com".
  - The new packages print the named brokers' own addresses. Please confirm.
  - IH-10 at FM 725 lists "Eldon Roalson, CCIM or Matt Howard" with eldon@ first. It is the only package without Bart Wilson; is that current?
  - Some original pages show old office addresses. If they are reused, they need replacing:
    - "2338 North Loop 1604 W." on the disclosure pages of 402 Nueva Street, 5001 Walzem Road and the River Walk site.
    - "18618 Tuscany Stone, Suite 200" on the aerials of 5001 Walzem Road, Cascade Caverns, IH 10 at Menger Springs and IH-35 at Wonderworld.
- **Exhibits drawn for Bill Miller Bar-B-Q projects on the land being offered.**
  - Kingsville: a November 2017 plan for a ±4,218 SF Bill Miller restaurant with drive-thru.
  - IH 10 East and Loop 1604: page 7, "BILL MILLER 1604 & I-10 … AERIAL MAP".
  - Loop 1604 / U. S. Highway 181: the July 2026 "BILL MILLER LOOP 1604 & US HWY 181" plan, which shows the offered 3.08-acre Lot 2 as a ±4,500 SF convenience store/gas station.
  - Should each stay in a buyer package? For Highway 181, please also confirm the offering is still an outright sale of Lot 2.
- **Outdated comments.**
  - River Walk site: "354 class A apartments. Opening 2024." The website repeats it.
  - IH 10 at Scenic Loop: "A Ford dealership is on the south side of the lots and a Circle K is on the west adjacent border". The current aerial shows Ford of Boerne across Scenic Loop Rd to the east and NAPA (sold Lot 4) between the site and Circle K, and only one lot is offered.
  - 503 & 523 Urban Loop Road: "HEB Headquarters is located one and a half blocks to the east", but the website says "one and a half miles". On the aerial it is about 0.3–0.4 mile away.

## Listing by listing

These notes apply to every listing:

- **Sale or lease.** 18 of the 22 originals never say "For Sale" or "For Lease", and the new covers print "For Sale" for them. Only 13810 Lookout Road and 25331 IH 10 West (For Lease), 5001 Walzem Road (For Sale) and Urban Loop (For Sale or Lease) state it. Tell us if any cover should read differently.
- **Traffic counts.** Every count was checked against TxDOT's 2025 counts. Only Comfort and San Marcos differ by more than 15% (see below). Three cited counts have no nearby 2025 station to compare: 77,301 at Scenic Loop, 53,870 at Dove Canyon and 10,657 on Old San Antonio Road.
- **Agency name.** Six traffic lines credit the "Texas State Highway Department", TxDOT's old name: 13810 Lookout, 402 Nueva, 5930 Bandera, IH 10 at Highway 46, IH 10 at Scenic Loop and IH 10 East and Loop 1604.
